Supply growth needed to break even = exp(cost per 10,000 PAPER ÷ income per 10,000 newly minted PAPER). Assuming that income ratio stays constant, total supply must grow by this multiple after minting for dividends to cover the cost. Values above 100× are labeled “Break-even threshold too high”; hover for the raw multiple. This is a display threshold, not a return forecast. Strategy considerations

  1. Use equal quantities of opposite positions in the same asset to reduce the combined loss of acquiring PAPER.
  2. When the current funding rate permits, receive funding on HL and include it in both outcomes.
  3. Use reward-index changes during continuous staking to assess dividends against issuance dilution and acquisition costs.

Both sides share Hyperliquid as a price source. Quoted differences require actual fills and final settlement to realize. Paper profit discounts, hard liquidation and submission delays can disrupt the ideal hedge.
